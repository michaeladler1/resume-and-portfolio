## Featured Projects

### 1. Zendesk Ticket Scoring & Solver Recommendation System

**Problem:** Manually evaluating agent contributions on complex, multi-touch support tickets led to inconsistent solver assignment and credit disputes. Identifying the right solver required subjective judgment across ticket history.

**Approach:** Built a multi-step Claude-powered workflow that automatically analyzes Zendesk ticket data, scores L2 agents based on specific contribution categories (solution, troubleshooting, escalation judgment, etc.), logs results to a persistent Google Sheets accuracy tracker, and recommends the most appropriate solver. The system iteratively refines scoring logic based on feedback.

**Impact:** Eliminates assignment disputes, provides objective scoring rationale, reduces time spent on solver selection decisions, and maintains an accuracy log for continuous improvement. Used weekly across the team.

### 2. Weekly GSL2 Meeting Prep Automation

**Problem:** Building weekly team meeting agendas required manually combing through Slack channels, emails, and notes to capture talking points, escalations, and action items. Process was time-consuming and often missed critical context.

**Approach:** Created an automated system that pulls recent Slack channel activity, identifies key themes (blockers, escalations, team updates), structures them into a prioritized agenda with talking points, and surfaces emerging issues that need discussion.

**Impact:** Saves 30–45 minutes per week on agenda preparation, ensures consistent meeting structure, captures items that might otherwise be missed, and enables more focused team discussions.

### 3. 1:1 Meeting Agenda Generator

**Problem:** Preparing for 1:1s with 30+ team members required manual review of individual activity. Lacked visibility into coaching opportunities, escalation patterns, or performance trends.

**Approach:** Developed a tool that automatically pulls Slack activity for a specific team member, surfaces recent contributions and interactions, detects escalation patterns and swarming behavior, and generates a structured agenda with coaching opportunities and discussion points.

**Impact:** Enables more informed, data-driven 1:1 conversations. Identifies coaching moments early. Detects escalation patterns that might signal skill gaps or workload issues.

### 4. AI Governance Framework for Team Operations

**Problem:** Rapid adoption of AI tools across the team created visibility gaps—multiple tools in use, unclear approval process, potential for redundant or ineffective tool sprawl.

**Approach:** Designed and implemented a comprehensive governance system including: (1) a Playbook documenting tool usage standards, (2) a Project Registry tracking all active tools and their owners, (3) an Intake Form for new tool requests with approval authority delegated to Team Leads. This creates a structured approach to tool adoption and prevents fragmentation.

**Impact:** Provides transparency on tool portfolio, prevents duplicative tool development, ensures tools are actively maintained and adding value, and establishes a repeatable process for future AI tool adoption.

---

### 5. Data-Driven L2 Minimum Services Policy

**Problem:** Support availability policies used an arbitrary 75% threshold that didn't align with operational reality, seasonal demand variance, or regional considerations. Led to fairness questions and didn't reflect actual business needs.

**Approach:** Conducted comprehensive analysis using Power BI, Workday, and shift schedule data across a confirmed 22-agent EMEA/Americas roster. Modeled seasonal demand patterns and created a tiered policy (PROTECT/STANDARD/FLEX) with day-of-week adjustments (Friday/Monday sub-rules) grounded in actual operational data.

**Impact:** Replaced arbitrary threshold with defensible, data-driven policy. Better aligns team expectations with business reality. Provides fairness in application across regions and enables predictable staffing planning.
